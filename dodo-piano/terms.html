Using Dodo Piano

The app is a musical toy for personal, family use. An adult should supervise use, choose a comfortable volume and manage purchases. It is not a professional music course and does not promise a particular educational or developmental outcome.

Free play and paid themes

Pink Garden is included free. Other themes are optional non-consumable in-app purchases: each selected theme is purchased once and has no recurring subscription charge. The App Store purchase sheet shows the applicable local price before confirmation. Buying one theme unlocks only that theme; it does not automatically include every theme or future content.

Purchase authorisation

Purchases are available from the adult area after a parent gate and are processed by Apple. The gate supplements Apple’s purchase controls; it does not replace device restrictions, Ask to Buy or adult supervision.

Restoring purchases

Eligible purchased themes can be restored after reinstalling or on another supported Apple device using the same purchasing Apple Account. Open the adult area and choose Restore Purchases. An internet connection and Apple authentication may be required. Local preferences are separate from purchases. Family Sharing applies only where the specific purchase is marked as eligible by Apple.

Request Apple-billed refunds through Apple’s refund service. Apple decides requests under its applicable policies and consumer law. A refunded or revoked purchase may no longer unlock its theme.

Availability and updates

Installed, unlocked themes can be played offline. Downloads, purchases and restoring access may require an internet connection and available Apple services. A one-time purchase has no scheduled expiry, but compatibility and availability depend on supported devices, operating systems and applicable store terms.
