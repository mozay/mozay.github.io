Support

Updated 4 October 2026

Help for grown-ups. We can help with sound, themes and restoring purchases.

Email support

Please include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version and a short description. Do not send your Apple password, payment details or a child’s personal information.

I reinstalled. Where are my themes?

Use the Apple Account that originally bought the themes. Open the lock button, complete the parent gate, then choose Restore Purchases. Connect to the internet and complete any Apple sign-in request. Purchases from a different Apple Account will not be restored to the current account.

A purchase is waiting for approval

Ask to Buy can require approval from a family organiser. The theme opens after Apple confirms the completed purchase. A pending or cancelled request does not unlock content.

I cannot hear the piano

Check the in-game sound button, device volume, silent mode and connected headphones or Bluetooth speakers. The app respects the device’s audio environment. Close and reopen the app if audio was interrupted.

Are all themes free?

Pink Garden is free. Additional themes are sold separately as one-time purchases from the adult area. You can preview them first. There is no subscription.

Does the game need the internet?

Installed free and unlocked themes work offline. Purchases, downloads and restoring access need a connection to Apple.

How do I request a refund?

Use reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple handles payment and refund decisions.
