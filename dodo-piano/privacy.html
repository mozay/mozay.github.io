Dodo Piano · For families

Privacy Policy

Updated 4 October 2026

Dodo Piano is a musical toy for young children. The app has no accounts, ads, tracking SDKs or developer-operated backend. Playing notes and using unlocked themes happens on the device.

For parents
  • We do not collect a child’s name, email, location, photos, voice recordings or playing activity through the app.
  • No advertising, behavioral tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple behind the parent gate.

Who is responsible

Dodo Piano is provided by Mehmet Özay, Türkiye. For privacy questions or requests, contact mehmetozay94@gmail.com.

Information on your device

The app stores preferences such as sound settings and the last selected theme locally. Apple’s StoreKit supplies verified transaction information so the app can determine which themes you own. We do not send this information to our own server. Local preferences may be removed when you delete the app; Apple’s purchase records are separate and can restore eligible purchases.

Apple purchases and platform services

Apple processes payments, purchase history, authentication and refund requests under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password. The app reads product identifiers and verified purchase status on the device. Apple may provide the developer with aggregate App Store sales and usage reports, and platform diagnostics according to your Apple settings. The app does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

The spoken parent prompt uses the device’s speech synthesis. The app does not activate the microphone, record speech or send a child’s voice to a server. Apple may manage voice downloads through its system services.

Children

The game is designed for young children with adult supervision. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. The parent gate protects purchasing and external links; it is not identity verification or consent to collect personal data. Please have an adult contact support, and do not send a child’s name, photos, voice or other unnecessary personal information.

If you contact support

When an adult emails us, we receive their email address, message and any information they choose to attach. We use it only to answer the request and resolve the issue. Our email provider processes this correspondence. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the request, manage an unresolved dispute or meet a legal obligation. We do not sell personal information or use support emails for targeted advertising.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, such as IP address and browser information, for hosting and security. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising pixels, forms or tracking cookies. External links are subject to the destination’s privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

You can reset local settings by removing the app. Restoring purchases uses the Apple Account that made the purchase. To request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to support, email mehmetozay94@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional data-protection rights, including the right to complain to a competent supervisory authority. We may retain information where legally required. For data held independently by Apple or GitHub, contact the relevant provider.

Changes

We will update this page and its date if these practices change. Introducing an account, analytics or another data-collecting feature would require an updated disclosure before that feature is released.
