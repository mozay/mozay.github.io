Dodo Piano

Updated 4 October 2026

Little fingers. Big tunes.

A bright piano playground with bouncing animals, ocean friends and colourful worlds. Tap a key and make your own music.

  • Start with the free Pink Garden piano.
  • Explore 17 optional themes with playful characters.
  • Preview a theme before a grown-up purchases it.
  • Play unlocked worlds offline, with no ads or accounts.

For iPhone and iPad. Additional themes are sold separately as one-time in-app purchases. Purchase controls and restoring access are in the parent area.
