Privacy policy

Last updated October 20, 2026 · Mehmet Ozay

Miyo is operated by Mehmet Ozay. Contact: mehmetozay94@gmail.com.

Using Miyo without an account

Miyo works without signing in. At first launch the app creates a random guest identifier with Firebase Authentication; it contains no name, email address or Apple ID. As a guest, your cats, journal entries, recordings, photos and videos stay only on your iPhone and are not sent to our servers. Recognising cat sounds while you listen runs on your iPhone with Apple's sound analysis.
Under the guest identifier our servers keep only what is needed for purchases: a purchase token for your App Store purchases and a keyed digest that ties purchases to this identifier. If you buy Miyo Pro as a guest, we also keep your subscription state, and RevenueCat keeps a customer record for this identifier. A guest without Miyo Pro cannot use Miyo's AI best guesses; the app asks you to sign in with Apple first.
Settings > Erase the journal on this iPhone removes everything stored on this iPhone and asks our server to delete the guest identifier and the records above. If the iPhone is offline, the local erase still happens and the guest identifier stays.

Signing in with Apple

You can sign in with Apple to back up your journal and use the AI features. Apple gives us an account identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and an email address (which can be Apple's private relay address). Firebase Authentication manages your sign-in; we never receive your Apple password. Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in method in the app; accounts created with Google in an earlier version keep the identifier, name and email Google shared, until the account is deleted.
When you are signed in, your cats, journal entries (mood, notes, detected sounds and saved best guesses), your active cat, and the recordings, photos and videos you save are stored in your Miyo account in Firebase (Google Cloud, region europe-west1 in the EU) so they follow you to a new phone. Only your account can read them. Deleting an entry removes it, and files no longer used by any entry, from your account. Device backups may also include this data according to your device backup settings.

AI best guesses

With your explicit AI permission, the selected recording, photo or six still frames taken from a video on your iPhone, the text you type, your cat's profile (name, personality and, if you entered them, breed description, sex and life stage), short earlier replies in the same conversation, and your language are sent through our server to OpenAI to create a best guess or a spoken reply. The full video and its audio are not uploaded. We send these requests to OpenAI with storage of the response at OpenAI turned off; OpenAI may still keep API data for up to 30 days under its abuse-monitoring policy (https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/). Uploaded media is processed in memory and is not saved to our database. The result (text, or the generated speech) is kept with a one-way digest of the request for 24 hours so a repeated request is not charged twice, then deleted automatically; Google's expiry deletion is asynchronous and may occur later. AI permission can be withdrawn in Settings.

Membership and purchases

Apple processes purchases and holds payment details; Miyo never receives card details. RevenueCat (United States) receives your account identifier (guest or signed in), your App Store purchase records and subscription status, to validate access and restore purchases (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/). Our server stores your subscription state (Pro status, product, expiry, renewal and billing-issue status) to unlock Miyo Pro. To stop the same purchase from being claimed by another account, we keep a record of each original App Store purchase with a keyed digest of the account that owns it; this record is kept after account deletion. If a subscription is shared with you through Apple Family Sharing, Apple tells our service that it is family-shared; we do not receive the purchaser's identity.

Notifications

Daily reminders and cat birthday reminders are scheduled on your iPhone and do not leave it. If you turn on Miyo updates, Firebase Cloud Messaging creates a push registration token for this installation and subscribes it to a general topic and a topic for your app language, so we can send news about new sounds and features. The token identifies the app installation, not your name or cat. Turning Miyo updates off unsubscribes and deletes the token; signing out does the same.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo or record a short video, and the microphone only while you record or listen. Nothing is captured in the background. Recordings are kept on your iPhone until you send, save or discard them.

Analytics and crash reports

Miyo uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand which features are used and to fix crashes. Analytics events name the feature and a count (for example that a recording was saved or a game was opened) together with your Firebase account identifier (guest or signed in), device model, OS version, app version and country; they never include your recordings, photos, text or your cat's name. Crash reports contain the technical state of the app at the time of a crash and your Firebase account identifier. This information is not used for advertising and is not shared with data brokers. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

Security and abuse prevention

Every request to our server must come with a valid sign-in and a Firebase App Check token created with Apple's App Attest, which lets our server confirm the request comes from the genuine Miyo app on a real Apple device; it does not identify you. To keep limits fair, we keep: daily usage counters stored under a keyed digest of your account identifier, deleted after 7 days (deleting and recreating an account does not reset them); timestamps of your requests in the last hour, deleted after 24 hours; per-minute purchase-check counters, deleted after 24 hours; and daily totals for the whole service, which contain no personal data. Infrastructure logs can include request time, IP address, path, status and technical identifiers and are kept for 30 days; our code does not log recordings, photos, generated text, passwords or keys.

Deleting your data

Signed in: Settings > Delete my account & data. Apple asks you to confirm it is you, then our server deletes your RevenueCat customer record, everything in your Miyo account (cats, entries, saved files and cached AI results), your subscription state, your purchase-token records and your sign-in account, and the app removes this account's data from your iPhone. Guests: see "Using Miyo without an account" above. You can also remove Miyo's access to your Apple ID in iPhone Settings > your name > Sign in with Apple.
What remains after deletion: a deletion marker with your former account identifier, kept for 30 days so requests still in progress cannot recreate deleted data; the purchase token itself without any link to your account; the purchase-ownership records and daily usage counters described above; webhook event identifiers and processing times, deleted after 30 days; infrastructure logs until they expire; analytics and crash data under Google's retention periods; and the transaction records Apple keeps for legal and accounting purposes. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell your personal data, show ads or track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app never asks for permission to track. Sharing a card or video uses the destination you choose in Apple's share sheet; that destination's privacy policy applies. Our service providers (Google, OpenAI, RevenueCat, Apple) may process data outside your country.

Entertainment

Miyo provides AI-assisted observations and uncertain interpretations. Best guesses, sound interpretations and reference sounds are not validated animal-language translations or veterinary advice. Breed, sex and life-stage details you enter only shape the tone and background of replies; Miyo never determines them from a photo or sound. Avoid uploading sensitive personal content or recordings of other people without permission.

Children

Miyo is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal data without appropriate permission, contact us to request deletion.
