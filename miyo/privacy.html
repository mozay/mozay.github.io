Privacy policy

Last updated September 9, 2026 · Mehmet Ozay

Miyo is operated by Mehmet Ozay. Contact: mehmetozay94@gmail.com.

Your account

Apple or Google sign-in provides an account identifier and, if you share them, your name and email. Firebase Authentication manages your sign-in. We use your account identifier to protect your AI requests and associate your membership. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
Google Sign-In may also process your IP address to estimate a general location for fraud prevention. Authentication SDKs can collect technical usage and diagnostic information to operate and improve their services. See https://developers.google.com/identity/sign-in/ios/app-privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

Photos, voice and stories

Cat profiles, saved photos, recordings and memories are stored on your device, separated by account and cat. They are not synchronized to other devices by Miyo. Device backups may include this data according to your device backup settings.
With your explicit AI permission, the selected photo, sampled video frames, recording or story text, your cat's profile and selected language are sent through our Firebase service to OpenAI to create a result or voice. For video observations, six still frames are extracted on your device; the full video and its audio are not uploaded. Raw uploaded photos, sampled frames and recordings are processed in memory and are not saved to our database. Generated responses and generated speech are temporarily cached, with deletion scheduled after 24 hours. Google's expiry deletion is asynchronous and may occur later. OpenAI may retain API data under its own abuse-monitoring and service-retention policies; see https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/ . AI permission can be withdrawn in Settings.

Membership and purchases

Apple processes purchases and holds payment details. RevenueCat receives your app account identifier, purchase records, subscription status to validate access and restore purchases. Miyo does not receive full payment card details. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/ .

Operational data

Firebase handles authentication, request security, subscription checks, quotas and service operation. Daily quota records use a keyed digest of your Apple or Google account identifier and expire after seven days, including if you delete and recreate your Miyo account. This prevents resetting daily limits. A keyed account digest associated with original purchase identifiers is retained to prevent another account from claiming the same purchase; the app never stores your payment card details. A limited deletion marker containing your former account identifier is scheduled for deletion after 30 days to prevent in-progress requests from recreating deleted data. Infrastructure logs can include request time, IP address, error status and technical identifiers. We do not intentionally log recordings, photos, generated text, passwords or provider keys.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline AI processing, revoke it in Settings, choose which photos or recordings to send, and delete individual memories or cat profiles. Settings > Delete my account & data deletes your Firebase account, app-generated cloud records and RevenueCat customer record, and removes local data for the current account on this device. Apple may retain transaction records for legal and accounting purposes. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings. You can contact us for privacy requests or help.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell your personal data or use advertising tracking. Sharing a card or video uses the destination you choose in Apple's share sheet; that destination's privacy policy applies. Our service providers may process data outside your country.

Entertainment

Miyo's cat replies are fictional entertainment. They are not validated animal-language translations or veterinary advice. Avoid uploading sensitive personal content or recordings of other people without permission.

Children

Miyo is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal data without appropriate permission, contact us to request deletion.

Changes

We may update this policy when functionality or providers change. The current version is available in the app and at this URL.
