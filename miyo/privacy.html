Privacy policy

Last updated September 14, 2026 · Mehmet Ozay

Miyo is operated by Mehmet Ozay. Contact: mehmetozay94@gmail.com.

Your account

Apple or Google sign-in provides an account identifier and, if you share them, your name and email. Firebase Authentication manages your sign-in. We use your account identifier to protect your AI requests and associate your membership. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
Google Sign-In may also process your IP address to estimate a general location for fraud prevention. Authentication SDKs can collect technical usage and diagnostic information to operate and improve their services. See https://developers.google.com/identity/sign-in/ios/app-privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

Photos, voice and stories

Cat profiles, saved photos, recordings, memories, lessons, reminders and health records are stored on your device and, when you are signed in, mirrored to your Miyo account in Firebase (Google Cloud, EU region) so they follow you to a new phone. Only you can read them; they are protected by your sign-in. Deleting a memory removes it from your account too, and deleting your account removes everything. Device backups may also include this data according to your device backup settings.
With your explicit AI permission, the selected photo, sampled video frames, recording or story text, your cat's profile (name, story personality and, if you entered them, breed description, sex and life stage), the short text of earlier replies in the same chat session, and your selected language are sent through our Firebase service to OpenAI to create a result or voice. Chat sessions are kept only in the app's memory while the screen is open, unless you save them to Memories in your account. For video observations, six still frames are extracted on your device; the full video and its audio are not uploaded. Raw uploaded photos, sampled frames and recordings are processed in memory and are not saved to our database. Generated responses and generated speech are temporarily cached, with deletion scheduled after 24 hours. Google's expiry deletion is asynchronous and may occur later. OpenAI may retain API data under its own abuse-monitoring and service-retention policies; see https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/ . AI permission can be withdrawn in Settings.

Membership and purchases

Apple processes purchases and holds payment details. RevenueCat receives your app account identifier, purchase records, subscription status to validate access and restore purchases. Miyo does not receive full payment card details. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/ .
If a subscription is shared with you through Apple Family Sharing, Apple tells our service that the subscription is family-shared; we do not receive the purchaser's identity.

Notifications

Daily reminders and cat birthday reminders are scheduled on your device and do not leave it. If you enable Miyo updates, Firebase Cloud Messaging creates a push registration token for this installation and subscribes it to a general topic and a topic for your app language, so we can send news about new sounds and features. The token identifies the app installation, not your name or cat. Turning Miyo updates off unsubscribes and deletes the token; signing out does the same. You can also disable notifications in iOS Settings. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo or record a short video, and the microphone only while you record. Nothing is captured in the background. Recordings must be at least three seconds and are kept temporarily on your device until you send, save or discard them.

Analytics and crash reports

Miyo uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand which features are used and to fix crashes. Analytics events name the feature and a count (for example that a chat turn or a game was opened) together with your Firebase user identifier, device model, OS version, app version and country; they never include your recordings, photos, chat text or your cat's name. Crash reports contain the technical state of the app at the time of a crash. This information is used only for app functionality and analytics, is not used for advertising and is not shared with data brokers. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy .

Operational data

Firebase handles authentication, request security, subscription checks, quotas and service operation. Daily quota records use a keyed digest of your Apple or Google account identifier and expire after seven days, including if you delete and recreate your Miyo account. This prevents resetting daily limits. A keyed account digest associated with original purchase identifiers is retained to prevent another account from claiming the same purchase; the app never stores your payment card details. A limited deletion marker containing your former account identifier is scheduled for deletion after 30 days to prevent in-progress requests from recreating deleted data. Subscription state is stored in Firebase to reflect membership changes and is deleted with the account. Pseudonymous short-term abuse-control records expire after one day. Webhook event identifiers and processing timestamps expire after 30 days to prevent duplicate processing. Infrastructure logs can include request time, IP address, error status and technical identifiers. We do not intentionally log recordings, photos, generated text, passwords or provider keys.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline AI processing, revoke it in Settings, choose which photos or recordings to send, and delete individual memories or cat profiles. Settings > Delete my account & data deletes your Firebase account, app-generated cloud records and RevenueCat customer record, and removes local data for the current account on this device. Apple may retain transaction records for legal and accounting purposes. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings. You can contact us for privacy requests or help.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell your personal data or use advertising tracking. Sharing a card or video uses the destination you choose in Apple's share sheet; that destination's privacy policy applies. Our service providers may process data outside your country.

Entertainment

Miyo provides AI-assisted observations and uncertain interpretations. Chat replies, sound interpretations and reference sounds are not validated animal-language translations or veterinary advice. Breed, sex and life-stage details you enter only shape the tone and background of replies; Miyo never determines them from a photo or sound. Avoid uploading sensitive personal content or recordings of other people without permission.

Children

Miyo is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal data without appropriate permission, contact us to request deletion.

Changes

We may update this policy when functionality or providers change. The current version is available in the app and at this URL.
