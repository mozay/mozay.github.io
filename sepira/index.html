The photograph in the drawer

Every family has one: creased, faded, a corner missing, the only picture of someone. Sepira works on that photograph.

Repair

Scratches, tears, creases and water stains are found and removed. The parts of the picture that survived are left alone.

Colour

Black and white and sepia pictures are given colour that fits the scene — skin, cloth, sky, room — so the photograph reads as a moment rather than a document.

Sharpen

Blurred and low-resolution scans are enlarged and sharpened, with the most care spent on faces, because that is what you are looking at.

Motion

From one still portrait, a short and deliberately small movement: a blink, the start of a smile. Enough that the person seems present, not enough to feel like a trick.
