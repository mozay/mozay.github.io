Getting started

What is mira.?

A personal stylist that works from the clothes you already own. Photograph your pieces and mira. builds your digital wardrobe, suggests outfits for your weather and plans, shows an outfit on a photo of you, and answers style questions in chat.

How do I sign in?

With Apple or with Google. Your wardrobe is tied to that account, so use the same one on every device. If you choose Apple’s Hide My Email, Apple gives us a relay address; signing in with Google later creates a separate account.

Can I use mira. in my language?

mira. speaks forty languages and follows your iPhone’s language. To use a different language for mira. only, open iPhone Settings → mira. → Language — or Profile → Language in the app, which takes you there. Your wardrobe and preferences stay the same.

Wardrobe

How do I add pieces?

In Wardrobe, tap +, then take a photo or choose up to twelve from your library. mira. removes the background and suggests a category and colours on your iPhone; check the details and correct anything before you save.

What makes a good photo?

One piece at a time, laid flat or on a hanger, in daylight, against a plain background that contrasts with it. Keep the whole piece in the frame.

I added pieces without a connection

They wait safely on your iPhone and upload by themselves when you are back online. Please do not delete the app before they have uploaded.

How do I edit or delete a piece?

Open the piece to edit its details, change its photo, mark it as in the laundry or stored away, or delete it. You can also press and hold a piece in the grid for quick actions.

What does “Wore it” do?

It records a wear. Wears power your wardrobe insights — most worn pieces, forgotten favourites and cost per wear — and help mira. rotate your outfits.

Outfits & weather

How does mira. choose outfits?

From the pieces you have available, the weather in your city, the occasion, your preferences and your reactions. Tell mira. what you liked or why something was not for you — it learns. See or forget what it learned in Profile → Style memory.

The weather is for the wrong city

Tap the temperature and city at the top of the home screen to choose another city. You can search for any city or use your location once; mira. keeps only the city.

Why am I not getting suggestions?

Outfits need a few available pieces — usually at least a top and a bottom, or a dress. Pieces marked as in the laundry, in repair or stored away are left out until you bring them back.

Virtual try-on

How does try-on work?

In the Studio, add a full-length photo of yourself, choose an outfit from your wardrobe and tap See it on me. The image is created by AI on our servers and usually takes under a minute.

Which photo works best?

Just you, standing and facing the camera, head to toe, in good light, in fitted clothes, against a plain background. Only use photos of yourself.

Is the result accurate?

It is an illustration of how the outfit might look — useful for deciding what goes together, but not a guide to size, fit or exact colour.

A try-on failed. Did I lose a credit?

No. When a try-on fails or is declined by safety filters, the credit comes back by itself — at the latest within about half an hour.

How do credits work?

One credit balance pays for Studio try-ons (1 credit) and for turning an outfit photo into wardrobe pieces (2 credits a photo). New accounts start with 3 welcome credits, and you do not need a membership to use them. Mira Plus adds 15 credits every month and Mira Pro 30 — yearly plans too, month by month. Each month they are topped back up to your plan’s amount; unused monthly credits do not stack on top, and the credits you have stay usable if your membership ends. You can also buy packs of 10, 25 or 50 credits that never expire: tap the credit counter in the Studio, or open Profile → Membership → Get credits.

Mira chat

What can I ask Mira?

Anything about getting dressed: what to wear, what goes with what, colours, occasions, packing and garment care. Mira knows your wardrobe and only talks about style.

Why did Mira say “see you tomorrow”?

Each plan has a number of messages per day: a few on the free plan, many more with Mira Plus or Mira Pro. The allowance refreshes every day.

Can I send a photo?

Yes — one per message, for example a piece you are thinking of buying or an outfit you want to refine. Mira describes the clothes only, never your body or face.

How do I delete a conversation?

Open History in the chat, choose the conversation and delete it. Its messages and photos are removed from our servers.

Planner & trips

How does the planner work?

Plan outfits for the days ahead, with the forecast beside each day, and set an optional reminder for the morning.

What is the smart suitcase?

With Mira Plus or Mira Pro, add a trip — destination, dates and what you have planned — and mira. builds a small capsule from your wardrobe, daily outfits and a packing list.

Membership

What is the difference between Mira Plus and Mira Pro?

Both include monthly credits, the smart suitcase and many more chat messages than the free plan. Mira Pro has everything in Plus, with twice the monthly credits and more messages a day. The membership screen shows exactly what each plan includes.

I paid, but mira. still shows the free plan

Open Profile → Membership and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same mira. account and the same Apple ID you paid with. Confirmation can take a minute. Still stuck? Write to us with the date of the purchase.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → mira. — or Profile → Membership → Manage subscription. You keep your membership until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, but please tell us what went wrong.

How does the gift wheel work?

Eligible members of the free plan can spin once per offer period, free of charge. The discount applies to the first period of a membership through the App Store and expires on the date shown. See the offer terms.

Account & privacy

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. After you confirm with Apple or Google, your wardrobe, looks, conversations, photos and sign-in are deleted right away. This cannot be undone and does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Who can see my photos?

Only you. They are stored privately in your account. AI providers receive a photo only when you use a feature that needs it. The Privacy Policy explains each case.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Write to us from the email address on your account and we will send it within thirty days.

How do I turn off notifications?

Profile → Notifications lets you choose which reminders you get. You can also turn them off in iPhone Settings → mira. → Notifications.
