Privacy Policy plainly written.

Last updated 28 September 2026 · Applies to the mira. app for iPhone and its services

mira. is a personal stylist that works from photos of your own clothes. That means it looks after things you care about — your wardrobe, your photos and sometimes a photo of you. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, who helps us process it, how long it stays and how to remove it.

In short

  • No ads, no tracking and no data brokers. Nothing is ever sold.
  • Your wardrobe, photos, conversations and plans live in your own account on Google Firebase in Frankfurt and can be read only through your sign-in.
  • AI features send only what they need, only when you use them, to Google’s Vertex AI, which may not use it to train models.
  • Delete any piece, photo, chat or try-on whenever you like. Deleting your account in Profile removes everything at once.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifier, no ad networks, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No contacts, microphone, health data or background location, and no calendar data on our servers.
  • No access to your whole photo library. mira. only receives the photos you pick, and saving an outfit card uses iOS’s add-only permission.
  • No face recognition, biometric templates or body measurements. Your photos are never used to identify you.
  • No payment card details — Apple handles payment.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared for anyone else’s marketing. Ever.

How the AI features use data

AI in mira. runs through our own servers. Data reaches an AI provider only when you use a feature that needs it, and only the part that feature needs.

Mira chat

When you send a message, our server sends Google’s Gemini model, through Vertex AI in the EU, your message, the last few messages of the conversation, your style preferences and city, up to forty wardrobe pieces as names, categories and colours (never their photos), your recent reactions, and the photo if you attached one. The reply is saved in your conversation.

Virtual try-on (Studio)

Before your first try-on, mira. asks you to confirm the photo is of you. Your photo and the photos of the pieces you chose are sent to Google’s Gemini image model through Vertex AI to create the image. Google may process this request outside the EU. The result is saved in your account until you delete it.

Photo to pieces

When you tap Create pieces, your outfit photo is sent to Google’s Gemini models through Vertex AI: in the EU to find and describe each piece you are wearing, and on Google’s global image service to create a new product image of each piece and check it against your photo, which may happen outside the EU. mira. does not keep the photo. The product images wait privately in your account for up to a day so you can review them; only the pieces you add are saved to your wardrobe, and the rest are deleted automatically. Hidden parts of a piece are estimated by the AI.

On your iPhone

Removing the background from a wardrobe photo, suggesting its category and colours, and choosing your daily outfit suggestions all happen on your iPhone, not on an AI service.

Google Cloud acts as our processor: it handles this data only to return a result to us. Google’s Vertex AI terms do not allow it to use your data to train its models. Providers apply safety filters, so some requests are declined; a declined try-on or photo does not use credits. Mira’s suggestions never have legal or similarly significant effects on you — ignore, change or reset them whenever you like.

Weather and location

  • Choosing a city. The city search uses Apple’s MapKit for the text you type.
  • Using your location. Only if you tap it: iOS asks for permission while using the app, your position is read once, Apple turns it into a city name, and mira. keeps only the city and country — never the coordinates. mira. does not follow your location in the background.
  • Forecasts. Weather comes from Apple WeatherKit for the centre of your chosen city, not your precise position. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.

Calendar and Siri

Calendar is optional. If you connect it, iOS asks for your permission and mira. reads the titles and times of your events for the next seven days, and your calendars' names, on your iPhone only, to suggest outfits. mira. never adds, changes or deletes events. Event details are not uploaded, not stored in your account or on our servers, not shared with anyone and never sent to AI providers. On your iPhone mira. keeps only this feature's settings — which calendars count and the occasion you chose for an event — and deletes them when you disconnect or sign out. If you plan an outfit for an event, only your usual plan is saved to your account: the date, the pieces, the occasion and any note you type yourself; the event title is never saved. Disconnect in Profile → Calendar, or remove access in iOS Settings.

Siri and Shortcuts. mira. offers shortcuts such as “What should I wear today”. Apple handles voice requests under its own privacy policy; mira. only learns which shortcut ran and opens that screen.

Notifications

Daily outfit and plan reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging issues a push token; we keep it with an installation identifier, your language, time zone and choices so messages arrive in your language at a sensible hour. If notifications are on, we also tell you when the mira. team has answered a support request; the notification never contains the answer itself. Turn news or all notifications off in Profile → Notifications or in iOS Settings. Signing out unlinks the token from your account.

Memberships and purchases

Apple processes every payment; we never see card details. RevenueCat, in the United States, receives your anonymous mira. account identifier and your App Store purchase records to confirm and restore purchases. Our server then records your plan, its dates and your credits. Only the server can change them — not the app on your phone. Offer codes are redeemed on Apple’s own page.

How long we keep it

  • Account, wardrobe, looks, plans and style memory — until you delete them or your account.
  • Conversations — until you delete the conversation, when its messages and photos are removed from our servers, or your account.
  • Try-on photos and results — until you delete them or your account.
  • Photo-to-pieces results you have not added — deleted automatically after one day; the photo itself is never stored.
  • Support requests and answers — kept with your account so we can answer you and you can see earlier requests; deleted with your account.
  • Usage counters and request records — kept with your account and deleted with it.
  • Push tokens — until you sign out, turn notifications off or the installation stops checking in.
  • Server logs — up to thirty days.
  • After you delete your account we keep only a record of the deletion holding a one-way hash of the old account identifier (never your email or content), the keyed hash used for one-time offers, and purchase records Apple and RevenueCat must keep for accounting and tax.

For speed, mira. also caches your photos on your iPhone; that copy disappears when you delete the app.

Deleting your data

  • One thing at a time. Delete a piece, a look, a trip, a conversation, a try-on photo or a result inside the app.
  • What Mira learned. Profile → Style memory lets you forget single reactions or reset them all.
  • Everything. Profile → Delete account. You confirm with Apple or Google, then our server deletes your account data, photos and sign-in straight away. You can also remove mira. from Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription — cancel it in your Apple ID subscriptions. You can also ask us by email to delete your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom (GDPR), Türkiye (KVKK) or California (CCPA) — you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable form and withdraw consent at any time. Most of your data can be seen, edited and deleted directly in the app.

For a copy of your data or any other request, write to mehmetozay94@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We answer within thirty days, free of charge. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Database and storage rules let an account read and write only its own records. Memberships, credits and offers can be written only by our server.
  • Paid AI features require Apple’s App Attest, a signed-in account, an active session and per-account limits.
  • We do not write message text, photos or sign-in tokens to our logs.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

mira. is not directed at children under thirteen, and we do not knowingly collect their data. You must be at least thirteen, or the age of digital consent where you live if that is higher. Upload only photos of yourself — never of other people or children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. If a change affects how your data is used, the app tells you before it takes effect.
