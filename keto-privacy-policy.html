02What we collect

Guest use

You can use the App without signing in. On first launch the App creates a guest account with a random identifier. We receive no name or email address for a guest account. What you save as a guest (the items listed under “Information you enter” below) is stored on our servers under that identifier.

As a guest you cannot rate recipes, and you cannot use the AI features (Panda Chef recipes, ingredient swaps, and label, meal and fridge scans) unless you have Pro. For these the App asks you to sign in with Apple.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you sign in with Apple, we receive Apple’s identifier for your account and the name and email address you choose to share. If you hide your email, we only receive Apple’s private relay address. Signing in from a guest account links Apple to that same account, so your saved data stays. If your Apple ID already has a Ketify account, the App switches to that account instead, and the guest data is not merged into it.

Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in method in the App. If you signed in with Google in an earlier version, we still hold the name, email address and profile photo address that Google shared at that time, until you delete the account.

Information you enter

  • Age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal and daily net-carb target
  • Dietary preferences, allergies, ingredients you avoid, eating style and cuisines you like
  • Meals you log, water and electrolyte check-ins, weigh-ins, and blood ketone and glucose readings
  • Pantry items
  • Favourites, cooking history, recipe ratings, and the recipes Panda Chef wrote for you (together with the ingredients you gave it)
  • Counters of how often you used free and AI features

Your shopping list, weekly meal plan and reminders stay on your device and are not sent to us.

Apple Health (only if you connect it)

If you connect Apple Health, the App reads your steps, active energy, resting heart rate and sleep, and writes the meals and water you log (energy, carbohydrates, fibre, protein, fat, water) and your weigh-ins to Health.

When you open your nutrition stats, the App saves the daily totals it read (steps, active energy, average resting heart rate and hours of sleep, one record per day) to your account on our servers, so your history is still there on a new device or after reinstalling. Individual Health samples are not uploaded.

Health data is used only to show you your own progress in the App. It is not used for advertising or marketing, not sold, not sent to the AI providers listed below, and not shared with any third party other than our hosting provider (Google Firebase). It is not shown to other users. You can stop the App reading or writing Health data with the Apple Health switch in the App or in the iOS Health app (Sharing › Apps › Ketify). Daily totals already saved stay in your account until you delete it.

Photographs

When you photograph (or choose from your library) a product label, your fridge or your plate, the image is sent through our servers to our image-recognition provider, fal.ai, only to read it. We do not store the photo: it is held only while the request is processed and is then discarded. The result (product name, nutrition values, recognised foods) is shown to you, and is saved to your account only if you add it to your log or pantry.

Voice commands

If you turn on voice control in cooking mode, the App uses Apple’s speech recognition, which may send the audio to Apple to recognise commands such as “next step”. We do not receive or store the audio or the recognised text.

Automatically collected

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): device model, operating system and app version, and technical details of the crash
  • Usage events (Firebase Analytics): for example that a screen was opened, a scan was used or the Pro screen was shown, together with device type, operating system, app version, language and approximate region. Analytics uses an app-installation identifier created by Firebase. It is not linked to your account and does not include the advertising identifier.
  • Push notifications: if you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns your device a push token so that we can send announcements. The token is held by Firebase, not in our database.
  • App integrity check: the App uses Firebase App Check with Apple’s App Attest service to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the App. This check does not give us any personal information.
  • Server logs: our server functions log technical events, which can include your account identifier.
What we never collect

Your location, contacts, the advertising identifier and your payment details never reach us. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we do not see your card details.

04Who processes it

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. To run the service we use these providers:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — authentication, database, server functions, file storage for catalogue images, crash reports (Crashlytics), usage analytics (Analytics, without the advertising identifier), push notifications (Cloud Messaging) and app integrity checks (App Check).
  • Google Gemini API — writes Panda Chef recipes and ingredient swaps. It receives the data listed for that purpose in section 3, never your name, email address, photos or Apple Health data.
  • fal.ai (USA) — reads labels, meals and fridge photos. It receives the photo, your language and, for fridge scans, the names of the foods recognised in it, and passes them to the AI model it runs. It never receives your name, email address or Apple Health data.
  • Pollinations.ai — recipe cover images. When a recipe has no stored photo, your device requests a generated image directly from Pollinations, using the recipe’s name and short description. Pollinations sees your device’s IP address and that text, but no account data.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status. It receives your account identifier and your App Store purchase information.
  • Apple — payments, Sign in with Apple, Apple Health (on your device), speech recognition for voice commands and App Attest.

We may also disclose data to competent authorities where the law requires it.

05Where it is stored, and for how long

Your account data (the database) and our server functions are located in the European Union. Some providers above, including parts of Firebase (authentication, analytics, crash reports, messaging), the Gemini API, fal.ai, Pollinations and RevenueCat, may process data in the United States or other countries outside the EU. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or another recognised safeguard.

06Deleting your data

In the App: Profile › Account › Delete account (for a guest: Delete my data). This permanently deletes, straight away and without a backup copy:

  • your profile and everything stored in your account, including the Apple Health daily totals and the recipes Panda Chef wrote for you;
  • your recipe ratings and any reports you sent;
  • any files that earlier versions of the App uploaded for you;
  • your customer record at RevenueCat;
  • your sign-in account.

The App also clears its local copies on your device. If you published a recipe to the shared catalogue in an earlier version, it stays there without any link to you; unpublished ones are deleted.

What remains after deletion:

  • If you used free AI features while signed in with Apple and without Pro, we keep one record: a keyed one-way hash (HMAC) of your Apple account identifier with the number of free AI uses you consumed per feature and the time of the last update. It cannot be turned back into your identifier and contains nothing else. It exists only so that deleting and re-creating an account does not reset the free AI allowance (abuse prevention, legitimate interest).
  • Records of processed subscription events, server logs and crash reports, until the periods in section 5 end.
  • Your purchase history at Apple, which Apple keeps under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings. You can stop using Sign in with Apple with Ketify at any time in iOS Settings › [your name] › Sign-In & Security › Sign in with Apple.

The App does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. It does not collect the advertising identifier, contains no advertising, and does not ask for tracking permission.

09Children

The App is not directed at anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from that age group. If we become aware of such data, we delete it.

10Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to the smallest number of people needed to run the service. No system is completely secure, however; in the event of a breach we will notify affected users and the competent authorities within the legally required timeframes.
