Questions

The short version. The app has the full FAQ.

Does Kaora replace my doctor?

No. Kaora turns official guidance into plain language. It does not diagnose or treat. When in doubt, ask your doctor or midwife.

My country is not in the list.

Pick it on the globe. You see your own country; the content comes from the WHO/EFSA based general list.

Where does my data live?

On Google Firebase, tied to your account. Health data stays on your device. You can delete everything from the Profile screen.

How do I cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kaora. Access continues until the end of the paid period.
