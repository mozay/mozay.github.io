What Kaora is

An app that tells you whether a food is considered safe in pregnancy, based on the public guidance of official bodies (BfR, ANSES, NHS, Voedingscentrum, ISS, AESAN, WHO/EFSA), with barcode scanning, plate and menu photo analysis and pregnancy-tracking tools. Some of it is free, the rest needs a subscription.

Photo and product notes are produced by artificial intelligence and may be wrong: a food can be misrecognised, an ingredient missed, a verdict mistaken. They are labelled as AI content in the app. Always check the packaging and official information.
