Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Kaora is a pregnancy food-safety app made by one person, for iPhone and Android. It asks for as little as it can, keeps it in one place and sells none of it. This page says exactly what is collected, why, where it goes and how to get rid of it. Where the iPhone app and the Android app work differently, both are described.

Who is responsible

Mehmet Ozay, an individual developer, is the data controller for Kaora on iPhone and Android. On Google Play the developer name is Keşkül Mobile Apps. Contact: support@keskulmobileapps.com.

Using Kaora without an account

Kaora works without signing up. At first launch the app creates a random guest identifier with Firebase Authentication. What you enter is stored on our servers under that identifier. The identifier is not your name, email address, Apple Account or Google account.

You can back up a guest account with Sign in with Apple (Profile → Account). On iPhone this uses Apple's built-in sign-in; on Android, Apple's sign-in page opens in a browser tab. The identifier stays the same, so your records and any subscription stay as they are, and you get them back on a new phone when you sign in with the same Apple Account. If that Apple Account is already backed up from another device, the app opens that account instead; what you entered as a guest on this phone is not merged into it. The app then has our server delete the guest account and what it stored on our servers, and tells you so. Logging out of a backed-up account leaves its data in that account and starts a new, empty guest on the phone.

A guest account exists only in this app on this phone. If you remove the app without deleting your data first, the guest account cannot be restored and we cannot tell which one was yours, so its data stays on our servers: the items with a time limit under "How long we keep it" are deleted automatically, and the rest is kept because we cannot link it to you. Use Profile → Delete all my data before removing the app.

As a guest you can use everything that does not use AI, on the same free and Pro terms. Plate and menu analysis and new AI product notes need Sign in with Apple or an active Pro subscription.

What we collect

  • Account identifier. If you back up with Apple: the identifier Apple gives us for Kaora, and your first name if you choose to share it. We do not ask Apple for your email address. Purpose: so your data follows you to a new device. Performance of a contract.
  • Pregnancy profile: due date (if you enter your last period instead, only the due date calculated from it is stored), your name if you enter one, height, pre-pregnancy weight, allergies including any you type yourself, country, units and language. Purpose: the features themselves. Explicit consent for health-related data; withdraw it by deleting your data.
  • Tracking entries: the weight you type in Kaora, water, caffeine, symptoms, kicks, contractions, appointments (title, place and time), questions for your doctor, hospital bag, reminder settings, saved foods and articles, and your recent barcode scans with the verdict shown. Purpose: the features themselves. Explicit consent for health-related data; performance of a contract for the rest.
  • Belly photos (Pro): the weekly photos you add, reduced in size and stored in our database with the week number and date, so they appear on every device you use and in the collage. Explicit consent. You can delete one photo or all of them in Belly Photos at any time.
  • Plate and menu photos and the answer we gave: the photo (reduced in size), the foods we recognised, the verdicts, your language and country. Purpose: so you can look them up later in "Plate history", and so we can show what we said if a question about a verdict ever arises. "Clear" in Plate history hides entries from your list; they stay on our server until they are deleted as described under "How long we keep it". Performance of a contract and legitimate interest.
  • Barcode scans: the barcode number, product name and brand, the product note we showed and the time. Purpose: to identify the product and keep a record of what we told you. The barcode is read on your phone; camera frames never leave it. Performance of a contract and legitimate interest.
  • Daily usage counters tied to your account, for example for Pro use, product lookups and, as a guest, barcode lookups.
  • A daily counter of free AI uses and barcode lookups per Apple identity, if you signed in with Apple. Its key is a shortened keyed hash (HMAC with a secret key held only on our server) of the identifier Apple gives us for Kaora. It contains no account identifier and no Apple identifier, and it stays when you delete your account (see "How long we keep it"), so that deleting and recreating an account does not reset the day's free uses.
  • A daily per-network counter. Its key is a shortened keyed hash (HMAC with a secret key held only on our server) of your IP address (for IPv6, of its /64 network prefix). Your IP address itself is not stored, and the counter is not linked to your account. Purpose of these counters: to apply the free limits fairly and stop abuse. Legitimate interest.
  • Subscription status, product and end date. Purpose: to know whether to unlock Pro. Performance of a contract.
  • App version, iOS or Android version, device model, language, country, whether the account is backed up and whether Pro is active, last launch time and launch count. Purpose: support and debugging. Legitimate interest in a working app.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): what the app was doing when it crashed, device model, iOS or Android version, app language and your random account identifier, so that a support request can be matched to a crash. Purpose: to find what broke. Legitimate interest.
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): which screens and features are used, and events such as a barcode scanned, a verdict category shown or the Pro page opened, together with device type, iOS or Android version, app version and the approximate country Google derives from the connection. Analytics does not receive your account identifier, barcodes, product names, photos or advertising identifiers. Purpose: to see what helps and what confuses. Legitimate interest; you can object by writing to us.

Apple Health (iPhone) and Health Connect (Android)

Kaora uses Apple Health on iPhone, or Health Connect on Android, only if you connect it (Profile → Apple Health on iPhone, Profile → Health Connect on Android). On Android, Kaora asks Health Connect for permission to read steps, active calories burned, distance, hydration and weight, and to write hydration and weight; you can allow or refuse each one.

  • Steps, active energy (active calories burned) and distance (on iPhone, walking and running distance) for the last seven days are read to show them in Statistics and Tracking. These values are read on your phone only. They are never uploaded or stored on our servers.
  • Water that other apps saved to Apple Health or Health Connect today is read and added to today's water count on screen. It is not uploaded.
  • With "Sync water and weight" on, body weight that other apps or scales saved to Apple Health or Health Connect in the last ninety days is read. A reading for a day that has no weight typed in Kaora is shown in your Kaora weight log and kept on this phone only. It is not uploaded and not stored with your account. Only the weights you type in Kaora are stored with your account, like your other tracking entries. Earlier versions of Kaora added these Health readings to your account; when the app reads Apple Health or Health Connect, a reading from the last ninety days that it finds in your account (same day, same value) is removed from our servers and kept on the phone only.
  • With "Sync water and weight" on, the water glasses (250 ml each) and weight you enter in Kaora are saved to Apple Health or Health Connect. When you remove a glass or enter a new weight for a day, the water or weight that Kaora itself saved for that day is removed there too.

Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never sent to OpenAI and never shared with anyone else. It is not read by people and not used to decide credit-worthiness or for lending. Kaora's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Turn off "Sync water and weight" or tap "Disconnect" in the same Profile screen to stop reading and writing. To withdraw the permissions themselves: on iPhone, open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Kaora; on Android, open Health Connect → App permissions → Kaora. Deleting your Kaora data does not remove the water and weight Kaora already saved to Apple Health or Health Connect; you can delete those there. Explicit consent.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. The app never asks for permission to track, and on Android the collection of the advertising ID is turned off.
  • No location, contacts or microphone. The camera and your photos are used only when you scan a barcode or take or choose a photo. On Android, photos are chosen with the system photo picker, which gives Kaora only the photo you pick.
  • No payment details. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes payments, depending on where you downloaded Kaora; we receive only the subscription product, whether it is active and when it ends.
  • No push tokens: reminders are scheduled on your phone, and widgets are updated on your phone, as are the Lock Screen Live Activities on iPhone and the ongoing notifications for the contraction and kick timers on Android.
  • When you share a week card or a summary, it goes through your phone's share sheet only to the app or person you choose.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared for anyone else's marketing. Ever.

Artificial intelligence

Plate and menu recognition, product notes and on-demand translation of Kaora's own food, week and article texts use the OpenAI API, called from our server. What is sent: the plate or menu photo; the product name, brand, ingredients, labels and nutrition values; Kaora's text to be translated; your language and country code; and, for recognition, the list of foods Kaora knows. Your name, email address, account identifier and Health data are never sent. Under OpenAI's API terms, API data is not used to train its models.

For a product with a known barcode, the note is generated from the product's Open Food Facts entry, not from anything typed or sent by your phone. It is stored with the product, so other users who scan the same product with the same country and language see the same note. This shared product store contains product data only, no personal data.

The AI features need Sign in with Apple, which gives limited free uses each day, or an active Pro subscription. A guest without Pro can see a product note or translation that already exists, but the AI does not generate a new one for them. Product information lookups by barcode do not use AI and stay open to guests, with daily limits.

AI answers can be wrong. They never replace official guidance or your doctor.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: Authentication (account identifiers), Cloud Firestore (database), Cloud Storage (plate and menu photos), Cloud Functions (our server code), App Check (genuine-app check), Crashlytics (crash reports) and Analytics (usage statistics). The database is stored in the European Union (multi-region in Belgium and the Netherlands); file storage and functions run in Belgium (europe-west1). Authentication, App Check, Analytics and Crashlytics are processed by Google globally, including in the United States.
  • OpenAI (AI analysis and translation) — United States.
  • RevenueCat (subscription state; receives your account identifier and your App Store or Google Play purchase records) — United States.

Each acts on our instructions. Transfers outside the European Economic Area rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Apple provides App Store payments, Sign in with Apple, Apple Health and the App Attest check under its own privacy policy. Google provides Google Play payments, Health Connect and the Play Integrity check under its own privacy policy.

Other services the app contacts

Open Food Facts (France) is an open product database. Our server looks up products there by barcode number only. If our server cannot be reached, your phone looks up the barcode number directly, and your phone loads product pictures from Open Food Facts. When your phone contacts Open Food Facts, it sees your IP address, as any website would. Nothing else is sent to it.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, tracking entries and belly photos — until you delete them or your data.
  • Barcode scans and the product notes we showed — until you delete your data.
  • Plate and menu photos with their answers — until you delete your data, at most three years, after which they are deleted automatically.
  • Daily usage counters tied to your account — ninety days, then deleted automatically.
  • Daily counters per Apple identity (keyed hash) — three days, then deleted automatically, also after you delete your account.
  • Per-network counters (keyed hash of the IP address) — seven days, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports — ninety days.
  • Usage analytics — event-level data for at most fourteen months; after that only aggregated statistics remain.
  • Subscription event records (the notifications our subscription provider sends us, kept so that a repeated notification is recognised) — deleted with your account, otherwise ninety days at most, then deleted automatically. They contain only a random account identifier, the product, the event type and dates.
  • Shared product data and product notes — as long as they are useful; they contain no personal data.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Türkiye you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, withdraw consent and receive it in a portable form. Under California law you have the right to know what is collected and to ask for deletion, and the right to opt out of sale — not needed here, because nothing is sold.

Delete everything inside the app: Profile → Delete all my data for a guest account, or Profile → Delete account for an account backed up with Apple. Both remove, on our servers: your profile, tracking entries, belly photos, plate and menu photos with their answers, barcode records, usage counters tied to your account, subscription status, subscription event records, your subscriber record at RevenueCat and the sign-in identity. On your phone they clear Kaora's stored data, widget data and running Live Activities (iPhone) or ongoing tracking notifications (Android), and the app starts again as a new guest. If your account is backed up with Apple, the app first asks you to confirm with Apple (on Android, Apple's sign-in page opens in a browser tab). To also remove Kaora's access to your Apple Account, go to iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple, or, on any device, account.apple.com → Sign-In and Security → Sign in with Apple.

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel an App Store subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings, and a Google Play subscription in the Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

If you no longer have the app, you can also ask us to delete your data: write to support@keskulmobileapps.com with the subject "Delete my data". Because Kaora does not know your email address, include your account ID if you have it (it is in every support email sent from the app). We delete the data listed above within thirty days.

What remains after deletion: the day's counter per Apple identity, which contains no account identifier and is deleted automatically after three days, so that deleting and recreating an account does not reset the free AI uses; per-network counters, which are not linked to your account and are deleted within seven days; crash reports until they expire; usage analytics, which are not linked to your account; and the shared product data, which contains no personal data. If the app cannot reach our server, nothing is deleted and the app asks you to try again.

For anything else write to support@keskulmobileapps.com. We answer within thirty days, free of charge. If you are unhappy with the answer you may complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

Kaora is not directed at children under sixteen and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and it will be removed.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Database and storage rules allow an account to read only its own records and to write only its own entries in the expected format. Usage counters, subscription status, the plate and menu archive and barcode records can be written only by our server, not by the app on your phone. Secret keys stay on our server; the app contains only the public client identifiers that Firebase and RevenueCat require.

To protect the AI features and free limits from abuse, the app shows our server that a request comes from the genuine Kaora app on a real device, through Firebase App Check: with Apple's App Attest service on iPhone, and with Google Play Integrity on Android. No personal data is collected for this check.
