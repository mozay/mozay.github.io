Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Kaora is a pregnancy food-safety app made by one person. It asks for as little as it can, keeps it in one place and sells none of it. This page says exactly what is collected, why, where it goes and how to get rid of it.

Who is responsible

Mehmet Ozay, an individual developer, is the data controller for Kaora. Contact: mehmetozay94@gmail.com.

Using Kaora without an account

Kaora works without signing up. At first launch the app creates a random guest identifier with Firebase Authentication. What you enter is stored on our servers under that identifier. The identifier is not your name, email address or Apple ID.

You can back up a guest account with Sign in with Apple (Profile → Account). The identifier stays the same, so your records and any subscription stay as they are, and you get them back on a new iPhone with the same Apple ID. If that Apple ID is already backed up from another device, the app opens that account instead; what you entered as a guest on this phone is not merged into it. Logging out of a backed-up account leaves its data in that account and starts a new, empty guest on the phone.

A guest account exists only in this app on this phone. If you remove the app without deleting your data first, the guest account cannot be restored and we cannot tell which one was yours, so its data stays on our servers: the items with a time limit under "How long we keep it" are deleted automatically, and the rest is kept because we cannot link it to you. Use Profile → Delete all my data before removing the app.

What we collect

  • Account identifier. If you back up with Apple: the identifier Apple gives us for Kaora, and your first name if you choose to share it. We do not ask Apple for your email address. Purpose: so your data follows you to a new device. Performance of a contract.
  • Pregnancy profile: due date (if you enter your last period instead, only the due date calculated from it is stored), your name if you enter one, height, pre-pregnancy weight, allergies including any you type yourself, country, units and language. Purpose: the features themselves. Explicit consent for health-related data; withdraw it by deleting your data.
  • Tracking entries: weight, water, caffeine, symptoms, kicks, contractions, appointments (title, place and time), questions for your doctor, hospital bag, reminder settings, saved foods and articles, and your recent barcode scans with the verdict shown. Purpose: the features themselves. Explicit consent for health-related data; performance of a contract for the rest.
  • Belly photos (Pro): the weekly photos you add, reduced in size and stored in our database with the week number and date, so they appear on every device you use and in the collage. Explicit consent. You can delete one photo or all of them in Belly Photos at any time.
  • Plate and menu photos and the answer we gave: the photo (reduced in size), the foods we recognised, the verdicts, your language and country. Purpose: so you can look them up later in "Plate history", and so we can show what we said if a question about a verdict ever arises. "Clear" in Plate history hides entries from your list; they stay on our server until they are deleted as described under "How long we keep it". Performance of a contract and legitimate interest.
  • Barcode scans: the barcode number, product name and brand, the product note we showed and the time. Purpose: to identify the product and keep a record of what we told you. The barcode is read on your phone; camera frames never leave it. Performance of a contract and legitimate interest.
  • Daily usage counters tied to your account, and a daily counter tied to a shortened one-way hash of your IP address (for IPv6, of its network prefix). The IP-hash counter is not linked to your account. Purpose: to apply the free limits fairly and stop abuse. Legitimate interest.
  • Subscription status, product and end date. Purpose: to know whether to unlock Pro. Performance of a contract.
  • App version, iOS version, device type, language, country, whether the account is backed up and whether Pro is active, last launch time and launch count. Purpose: support and debugging. Legitimate interest in a working app.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): what the app was doing when it crashed, device model, iOS version, app language and your random account identifier, so that a support request can be matched to a crash. Purpose: to find what broke. Legitimate interest.
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): which screens and features are used, and events such as a barcode scanned, a verdict category shown or the Pro page opened, together with device type, iOS version, app version and the approximate country Google derives from the connection. Analytics does not receive your account identifier, barcodes, product names, photos or advertising identifiers. Purpose: to see what helps and what confuses. Legitimate interest; you can object by writing to us.

Apple Health

Kaora uses Apple Health only if you connect it (Profile → Apple Health).

  • Steps, active energy and walking and running distance for the last seven days are read to show them in Statistics and Tracking. These values are read on your phone only. They are never uploaded or stored on our servers.
  • Water that other apps saved to Health today is read and added to today's water count on screen. It is not uploaded.
  • With "Sync water and weight" on, body weight that other apps or scales saved to Health in the last ninety days is read. A reading for a day that has no Kaora entry is added to your Kaora weight log. Your weight log is stored with your account like your other tracking entries.
  • With "Sync water and weight" on, the water glasses and weight you enter in Kaora are saved to Health.

Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never sent to OpenAI and never shared with anyone else. Turn off "Sync water and weight" or tap "Disconnect" in Profile → Apple Health to stop reading and writing. To withdraw the permissions themselves, open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Kaora. Explicit consent.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. The app never asks for permission to track.
  • No location, contacts or microphone. The camera and your photo library are used only when you scan a barcode or take or choose a photo.
  • No payment details. Apple processes payments; we receive only the subscription product, whether it is active and when it ends.
  • No push tokens: reminders are scheduled on your phone, and Lock Screen Live Activities and widgets are updated on your phone.
  • When you share a week card or a summary, it goes through the iOS share sheet only to the app or person you choose.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared for anyone else's marketing. Ever.

Artificial intelligence

Plate and menu recognition, product notes and on-demand translation of Kaora's own food, week and article texts use the OpenAI API, called from our server. What is sent: the plate or menu photo; the product name, brand, ingredients, labels and nutrition values; Kaora's text to be translated; your language and country code; and, for recognition, the list of foods Kaora knows. Your name, email address, account identifier and Health data are never sent. Under OpenAI's API terms, API data is not used to train its models.

For a product with a known barcode, the note is generated from the product's Open Food Facts entry, not from anything typed or sent by your phone. It is stored with the product, so other users who scan the same product with the same country and language see the same note. This shared product store contains product data only, no personal data.

AI answers can be wrong. They never replace official guidance or your doctor.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: Authentication (account identifiers), Cloud Firestore (database), Cloud Storage (plate and menu photos), Cloud Functions (our server code), App Check (genuine-app check), Crashlytics (crash reports) and Analytics (usage statistics). The database is stored in the European Union (multi-region in Belgium and the Netherlands); file storage and functions run in Belgium (europe-west1). Authentication, App Check, Analytics and Crashlytics are processed by Google globally, including in the United States.
  • OpenAI (AI analysis and translation) — United States.
  • RevenueCat (subscription state; receives your account identifier and your App Store purchase records) — United States.

Each acts on our instructions. Transfers outside the European Economic Area rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Apple processes payments, Sign in with Apple, Apple Health and the App Attest check under its own privacy policy.

Other services the app contacts

Open Food Facts (France) is an open product database. Our server looks up products there by barcode number only. If our server cannot be reached, your phone looks up the barcode number directly, and your phone loads product pictures from Open Food Facts. When your phone contacts Open Food Facts, it sees your IP address, as any website would. Nothing else is sent to it.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, tracking entries and belly photos — until you delete them or your data.
  • Barcode scans and the product notes we showed — until you delete your data.
  • Plate and menu photos with their answers — until you delete your data, at most three years, after which they are deleted automatically.
  • Daily usage counters tied to your account — ninety days, then deleted automatically.
  • IP-hash counters — seven days, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports — ninety days.
  • Usage analytics — event-level data for at most fourteen months; after that only aggregated statistics remain.
  • Subscription event records — as long as tax and accounting law requires them; they contain only a random account identifier, the product and dates.
  • Shared product data and product notes — as long as they are useful; they contain no personal data.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Türkiye you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, withdraw consent and receive it in a portable form. Under California law you have the right to know what is collected and to ask for deletion, and the right to opt out of sale — not needed here, because nothing is sold.

Delete everything inside the app: Profile → Delete all my data for a guest account, or Profile → Delete account for an account backed up with Apple. Both remove, on our servers: your profile, tracking entries, belly photos, plate and menu photos with their answers, barcode records, usage counters, subscription status, your subscriber record at RevenueCat and the sign-in identity. On your phone they clear Kaora's stored data, widget data and running Live Activities, and the app starts again as a new guest. If your account is backed up with Apple, the app first asks you to confirm with Apple. To also remove Kaora's access to your Apple ID, go to iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

Deleting your data does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

What remains after deletion: subscription event records needed for tax and accounting law, which contain only the deleted random account identifier, the product and dates; IP-hash counters, which are not linked to your account and are deleted within seven days; crash reports until they expire; usage analytics, which are not linked to your account; and the shared product data, which contains no personal data. If the app cannot reach our server during deletion, it deletes what it can directly; write to us and we will delete the rest.

For anything else write to mehmetozay94@gmail.com. We answer within thirty days, free of charge. If you are unhappy with the answer you may complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

Kaora is not directed at children under sixteen and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and it will be removed.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Database and storage rules allow an account to read only its own records and to write only its own entries in the expected format. Usage counters, subscription status, the plate and menu archive and barcode records can be written only by our server, not by the app on your phone. Secret keys stay on our server; the app contains only the public client identifiers that Firebase and RevenueCat require.

To protect the AI features and free limits from abuse, the app shows our server that a request comes from the genuine Kaora app on a real Apple device, using Apple's App Attest service through Firebase App Check. No personal data is collected for this check.
