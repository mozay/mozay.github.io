Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026 · Lenara

This policy explains what data Lenara collects, why it collects it, and what happens to it. We kept it short, because a policy nobody reads is worth nothing.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and nameTo identify your account and carry your favourites between devices. When you sign in with Apple or Google, they provide this.
Profile photoOnly when signing in with Google. Shown inside the app and sent nowhere else.
Your favourites and listening historySo you can pick up where you left off and find your library on a new device.
Subscription statusTo determine your access to Pro content.
Daily listen countTo enforce the free usage limit. We count how many times you listened, not what you listened to.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect an advertising identifier and we don't show ads.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't ask for your location, contacts, photos or health data.
  • We don't sell your data. Not under any circumstances.

Where your data lives

Your account details and preferences are stored on Google Firebase, on servers inside the European Union (europe-west1). Audio files are stored on Cloudflare R2 and reached only through short-lived, personal links.

Children

Lenara is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

How to exercise your rights

To request a copy of your data or to have your account deleted entirely, write to mehmetozay94@gmail.com. We complete deletion requests within 30 days; deletion cannot be undone, and your favourites and history go with it.

Changes

If this policy changes we update the date, and we tell you inside the app when the change is significant.
