The sound stops when the screen turns off

Settings → Lenara → Background App Refresh must be on, and Low Power Mode must be off. If it keeps happening, write to us.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lenara → Cancel. You stay Pro until the end of the period.

My subscription doesn't show on a new device

Sign in with the same account, then restore purchases from the Profile screen.
