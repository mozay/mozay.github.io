A calmer night starts here

Sleep stories, guided meditations and ambient sound — in nineteen languages, each with stories written in that language.

Sleep stories

Over a thousand stories, around seventeen minutes each, read slowly and softly by a voice that is trying not to wake you. A whisper narration is available too.

Guided meditation

Sorted by what you actually need tonight: falling asleep, anxiety, a body scan, breathing, focus, letting go of tension.

Ambient sound

Rain, waves, fire, forest and coloured noise, generated on your phone. They never loop and use no data.

A timer that fades

When the time is up the sound does not cut off — it fades over the last half minute, and the screen dims.

Works offline

Download the audio and the text. On a plane, on the underground, with no signal at all.

Nineteen languages

Not one library translated nineteen times. Each language has its own stories, with its own places and weather.
