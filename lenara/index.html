A sleep plan, not just a timer

Choose how many stories play tonight and what follows: nothing, or fifteen minutes to a whole night of ambience picked from your favorites. The last story fades in its final half minute, then the screen dims. Lenara remembers the plan for every night after.

1

How many stories

One, two, three, four, or unlimited. Repeat a single story as many times as you like.

2

What comes after

Rain, a night train, crickets. Picked from your favorite sounds, a different one each night.

3

Then the night ends

A slow fade instead of a cut. The screen darkens for a calmer nightstand.

Questions

Are the stories really written in each language?

Yes. Each language has its own set of stories with its own settings; nothing is translated from an English original. The narration is recorded per language as well.

Do the sounds use data or battery?

No data: the sounds are synthesized on the device. Battery use is similar to playing a music file.

Can I use it offline?

All 53 sounds work offline. With Pro, stories and meditations can be downloaded for offline listening.

How does the subscription work?

Lenara Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, weekly, monthly or yearly, billed through your App Store account. Cancel anytime in your App Store settings. Family Sharing is supported.

How do I get help?

Write to us from the app (Profile → Contact us) or through the support page. Your user ID is at the bottom of the Profile screen.
